Home TV Gallery

Turn a Samsung TV into a living art gallery and a calm family dashboard, run by a spare Windows laptop. About 45 minutes, no coding.

What you get
  • 🎨 Gallery mode: masterpieces from public museums' open-access collections, framed with a short note about each work. The art also sits softly behind the dashboard.
  • ☀️ The TV can switch itself on at times you choose, with a short spoken greeting, weather, your day's calendar and music (devotional music early in the morning if you like, instrumental jazz otherwise).
  • 🕒 Clock, weather, sunrise/sunset, world clocks, exchange rates and 22K gold price, headlines, a kids' chores board.
  • 🗺️ Optional: live commute map with traffic (needs a free Google Maps key).
  • 🎬 Optional: film posters mixed into the gallery (needs a free TMDB key; paste it as tmdbKey in C:\HomeTV\app\js\config.js).
  • 🧩 Extras for the more adventurous: “on the way home” map from Find My, deliveries & bills from Gmail, and a family photo frame. See extras/README.md.
  • 🔧 Looks after itself: restarts anything that stops and keeps the laptop awake.

What you need

  • A Samsung smart TV from 2017 or later (Tizen; most Samsung TVs with the colourful home bar).
  • A Windows 10 or 11 laptop/PC that can stay plugged in and switched on near your router, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  • About 10 GB of free disk space.

Setup

1

Install Docker Desktop on the laptop

Download it free from docker.com/products/docker-desktop and install with the default options. Restart when asked, open Docker Desktop once, accept its terms and skip signing in. Wait until the bottom-left corner says Engine running.

Tip: Setup.cmd can also install it for you – it will tell you to restart and run it again.

2

Prepare the TV (2 minutes)

  1. Switch the TV on.
  2. Settings → General → Network → Expert Settings → Power On with Mobile → On (on some models: Settings → Connection → Expert Settings). This lets the laptop switch the TV on.
  3. Leave Developer Mode for now – the setup wizard will tell you exactly when and what to type.
3

Run the setup wizard

Unzip this folder anywhere, then double-click Setup.cmd and click Yes when Windows asks for permission. The wizard:
  • finds your TV on the Wi-Fi,
  • asks a few simple questions (your name, city, weekend days, currencies, switch-on times, optional calendar links),
  • sets the laptop to stay awake, installs everything, and puts the app on your TV.
Developer Mode (the wizard will pause for this): on the TV open Apps, then type 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote (no number keys? press the 123 button to bring up the on-screen numbers). Switch Developer mode ON, enter the Host PC IP the wizard shows you, press OK and restart the TV (hold the power button until the Samsung logo appears).
4

Let the laptop sign in by itself (recommended)

After a Windows update restart, nothing runs until someone signs in. To avoid that, download Autologon from Microsoft (learn.microsoft.com/sysinternals/downloads/autologon), run it, type your Windows password yourself and press Enable.

Only do this on a laptop that stays at home – anyone who opens it will be signed in.

That's it. Switch the TV off and on – within about 15 seconds the dashboard opens and greets you.

Optional extras

Google Calendar

On a computer open Google Calendar → ⚙ Settings → click your calendar → scroll to Secret address in iCal format → copy. Run Setup.cmd again (option 1) and paste it when asked. Repeat for each calendar. Keep these links private.

Live commute map

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com, create a project and turn on billing (a card is required, but normal home use stays well inside Google's free monthly allowance – add a $1 budget alert to be safe).
  2. Enable Routes API and Maps Static API.
  3. APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → API key. Restrict it to those two APIs.
  4. Run Setup.cmd (option 1), paste the key and your home and office addresses.

Changing anything else

All settings live in C:\HomeTV\app\js\config.js (world clock cities, app row, art themes, music stations, countdowns, kids' tasks…). Edit it in Notepad, then run Setup.cmd and choose option 2 to reinstall with your edits.

If something isn't working

TV doesn't switch on by itselfCheck Power On with Mobile is on and the TV is plugged in at the wall. See C:\HomeTV\wake-log.txt.
Dashboard doesn't open at switch-onMake sure Docker Desktop is running on the laptop. After a big TV update Developer Mode can switch off – turn it on again (step 3 tip) and run Setup.cmd → option 2.
No greeting voice or calendarThe laptop's address may have changed – run Setup.cmd → option 1. Reserving the laptop's address in your router prevents this.
Anything elseLook at C:\HomeTV\health-log.txt, or just run Setup.cmd again – it is safe to repeat.
